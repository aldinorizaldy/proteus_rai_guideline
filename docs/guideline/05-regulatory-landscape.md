# Regulatory Landscape

This section maps the legal and normative frameworks that shape responsible AI in PROTEUS and states how each one touches project work. PROTEUS commits to full alignment with the GDPR, the principles of the AI Act and the European Code of Conduct for Research Integrity, and applies the same governance framework to its non-EU partners, Sundosoft Ltd. and the Korea Rural Community Corporation, so that cross-border collaboration meets both EU and relevant South Korean regulations. Because regulations and standards keep evolving, they are tracked throughout the project; the tracking and escalation arrangements are described in the governance section. The provision-by-provision mapping is held in the standards crosswalk annex.

## EU AI Act

The EU AI Act is the binding European regulation for AI systems and follows a risk-based approach. This subsection summarises the obligations that can apply to PROTEUS outputs, notably for general-purpose and foundation models (documentation, transparency, risk assessment and security) and for AI used in sensitive domains such as migration monitoring (UC1) or civil protection. It describes how the project's models and use cases are assessed against the Act's categories and which parts of this guideline (principles, risk register, metadata schema, compliance process) serve as evidence of conformity. Because the research and prototype status of the work affects which obligations apply, it also notes where official guidance is still developing and how changes are followed.

## ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42

ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 is the joint ISO and IEC subcommittee that develops international standards for artificial intelligence, including terminology, AI management systems, risk management, trustworthiness, bias and data quality. This subsection identifies the SC 42 standards that are relevant to AI4EO and FM development and relates their concepts to the principles of this guideline, so that PROTEUS terminology and evaluation criteria remain compatible with international practice. The mapping is intended to feed the standards crosswalk table planned as an annex to this guideline.

## IEEE P7000

The IEEE P7000 series addresses ethical considerations in the design of autonomous and intelligent systems, with individual standards on topics such as ethical system design, transparency, data privacy and algorithmic bias. PROTEUS draws on the series, together with IEEE's Ethically Aligned Design, as a source of design-time practices. This subsection lists the parts of the series that inform the principles on transparency, fairness, privacy and social impact, and records the link to the IEEE GRSS community through which HZDR can contribute to standards work in AI4EO.

## ITU-T

ITU-T recommendations and the related ITU work on AI give technical guidance on the use of AI for environmental monitoring and disaster management, on the environmental efficiency of AI, and on interoperability. This subsection records the recommendations that PROTEUS follows, as named in the task description of T1.4 & T2.4, and indicates how they relate to requirements for Copernicus services such as interoperability, quality and trust in AI-generated information.

## GDPR

The General Data Protection Regulation governs the processing of personal data and applies to PROTEUS wherever data can relate to identifiable persons. The project works with non-personal or anonymised data, applies privacy, security and data minimisation, and stores data in line with FAIR and INSPIRE standards. This subsection describes the GDPR touchpoints: lawful basis and consent where applicable, anonymisation and re-identification risk for high-resolution EO and in-situ data, licensing and intellectual property, and cross-border transfers involving non-EU partners. It is coordinated with the Data Management Plan (D1.2 and D2.1) led by SERCO.
