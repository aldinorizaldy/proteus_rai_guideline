# Compliance Assessment Process

This section defines how project outputs are checked against the guideline. It describes a self-assessment workflow in which each partner completes a compliance card at three lifecycle stages (data collection, model training, and reporting or deployment), giving the status of each parameter together with a justification and evidence. It sets out the trigger points at which an assessment is required, the roles involved (the submitting partner, the RAI lead at HZDR as reviewer, and the External Advisory Board for escalation), how results are logged and shared across partners, and how the findings feed the evaluation of KR06 across UC1 to UC7. Results are exported in machine-readable form so that they can be aggregated and audited.
