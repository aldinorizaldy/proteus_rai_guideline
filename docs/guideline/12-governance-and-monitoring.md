# Governance and Monitoring

This section sets out how the guideline is kept current and enforced. It covers the tracking of standards and regulatory developments (EU AI Act guidance, ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42, IEEE and ITU-T), the monitoring and auditing of project activities against the guideline, and the audit cadence, planned in two phases (M15 to M18 and M28 to M30). It defines the escalation path for unresolved or serious findings, including the External Advisory Board, which reviews responsible AI integration and compliance with EU ethics requirements, and the change management of the guideline and the metadata schema across versions. It also states how lessons learned feed into the next version of the guidelines (D2.2) and the next model release. It also defines decision rights at release: who may approve, defer or block the release of a model or dataset, how exceptions are requested and recorded, and how disagreements are escalated.
