# Annex I: PROTEUS Project Context and Commitments

This annex records the PROTEUS context that the core guideline leaves out. The guideline is deliverable D1.3 (version 1, M10) of PROTEUS, produced under the Responsible AI4EO, ethics and standards task (T1.4 & T2.4) led by HZDR, and it is revised into D2.2 (M36). The core dimensions named in the T1.4 & T2.4 task description (transparency, fairness, privacy, robustness, energy efficiency and social impact) and the foundation model risks it names are the origin of the principles and risk sections; uncertainty and reliability was added as a separate principle in line with the focus of T3.4 & T4.4, and human oversight and agency was added in this version. The guideline is linked to the technical work on responsible, secure and uncertainty-aware foundation models in T3.4 & T4.4 (Key Result KR06), which develops the privacy-preserving training, uncertainty quantification and validation methods that the strategies refer to, including out-of-distribution detection by comparing embeddings, led by WUR on UC2 data, and to the communication of uncertainty to users of the Copernicus Agent. For energy efficiency it links to the GPU optimisation work led by NVIDIA (T9.6 & T10.5) and the efficiency benchmarking in KR26. On regulation, PROTEUS commits to full alignment with the GDPR, the principles of the AI Act and the European Code of Conduct for Research Integrity, and applies the same governance framework to its non-EU partners, Sundosoft Ltd. and the Korea Rural Community Corporation, so that cross-border collaboration meets both EU and relevant South Korean regulations. The project works with non-personal or anonymised data stored in line with FAIR and INSPIRE standards, follows the ITU-T recommendations named in the task description, and can contribute to AI4EO standards work through HZDR's link to the IEEE GRSS community.
